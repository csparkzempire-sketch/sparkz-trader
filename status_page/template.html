Research behind these accounts

Full-period backtests of the same rules on each market, with that market's spread and slippage. Markets marked Paper trading passed and are running live; the rest were tested and not added.

PF is profit factor: gross profit divided by gross loss. Profitable periods counts six-week windows (hourly) or calendar years (daily) in which buy-only made money.

All variations use the same stretch of history, mostly rising for crypto and gold. This shows the strategy is not fragile; it does not show it will keep working, which is what the paper accounts are for.

Paper trading only: simulated fills with realistic spread, slippage and risk limits; no orders reach a real broker. Backtests and a few weeks of paper results do not guarantee future performance. Updated hourly by the Sparkz Trader paper-trading run.
